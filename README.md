<br>

Windows 11 with Ubuntu 24.04 under WSL2. TypeScript, Rust, C++, Python. I build
things that hold state and keep running after I close the laptop.

Right now that means an intelligence that grows its own internal structure instead
of inheriting someone else's checkpoint, a scheduled analyst that reads energy
markets and takes its own positions, and the interfaces those things get seen
through. Some of it is research that may never resolve. That is an acceptable
outcome; a polished answer assembled from templates is not.

Most of the work is private, which is why the map has more dark nodes than lit
ones. The lit ones are real. So are the dark ones.

Rules I do not break:

- every line of code needs a reason to exist
- a bug fix is not an invitation to refactor
- nothing is done until it is verified on the deployed surface
- superseded means deleted, not archived in place
